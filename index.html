About Us

Michel Keck is a contemporary artist who creates original abstract paintings and fine art prints for collectors, interior designers, art consultants, and design firms. Her artwork brings personality and a distinctive artistic presence to homes, hotels, offices, and other carefully designed spaces.

Michel works directly with clients to help them find artwork that matches their vision and style. Collectors and design professionals can commission original pieces or choose from available paintings and print editions. To explore her artwork and learn more, visit the official Michel Keck website.

Along with creating original artwork, Michel collaborates with brands on art licensing and surface design. These partnerships bring her abstract designs to different products and materials, offering creative ways to enjoy her work.
